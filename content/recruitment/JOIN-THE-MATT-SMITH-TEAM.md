## Why this message works (positioning notes)

1. **It leads with the agent's pain, not our trophy case.** Agents are pitched constantly. Opening with *"here are the keys, good luck"* names the exact frustration they already feel — so they keep reading.
2. **The tech is our unfair advantage.** Almost no local team has a custom-built Command Center. Naming it (leads, automated follow-up, deadline-proof transactions, hands-off marketing) makes the offer concrete and impossible to copy.
3. **It speaks to two audiences in one breath.** Newer agents hear *training + leads + a playbook.* Experienced agents hear *leverage + less chaos + more net.* The "Who this is for" section lets each one self-select.
4. **It sells the net, not the split.** Reframes the commission conversation: when leads, tech, marketing, and admin are included, take-home is what matters — and that's a conversation, not a number on a flyer.
5. **The ask is tiny.** "One honest conversation," "compare notes," "no pitch." Low commitment gets the meeting; the systems and numbers close it.
